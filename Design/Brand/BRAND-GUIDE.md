# Coast visual identity

## Brand idea

The three strokes progress in length and intensity. They represent individual wheel impulses being transformed into increasingly fluid motion.

The strokes share one left edge. Their increasing length reads as forward momentum; do not center the shorter strokes over the longest stroke.

## Primary colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight Ink | `#10143D` | Wordmark, dark logo, primary text |
| Deep Indigo | `#111442` | App-icon foundation and dark surfaces |
| Coast Violet | `#9A7CFF` | First impulse and highlight |
| Momentum Blue | `#6068FF` | Middle impulse and primary accent |
| Deep Current | `#2F49F5` | Longest impulse and active state |
| Mist | `#EEF1F8` | Quiet backgrounds and dividers |

## Clear space

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of its middle stroke. Do not place text or container edges inside that area.

## Minimum sizes

- Standalone digital mark: 24px tall
- Horizontal lockup: 120px wide
- Menu-bar template: use the supplied 18pt asset

At sizes below 24px, use the monochrome mark. Do not use three separate brand colors at menu-bar size.

## Correct use

- Use the color mark on white, Mist, or very light neutral backgrounds.
- Use the ink mark on light backgrounds.
- Use the white mark on Midnight Ink or sufficiently dark imagery.
- Use only the supplied template glyph in the macOS menu bar.
- Preserve the order and relative lengths of all three strokes.
- Keep all three strokes left-aligned.

## Avoid

- Do not rotate the mark.
- Do not add outlines, bevels, or drop shadows to the standalone logo.
- Do not make all three strokes equal length.
- Do not place the full-color mark in the macOS menu bar.
- Do not recolor individual strokes outside the approved palette.
- Do not compress or stretch the mark.
