A calmer way to scroll

Made for wheels.
Respectful of everything else.

Coast smooths the input that needs it and steps aside when macOS is already receiving continuous motion.

01

Momentum, not mush

Discrete wheel ticks become a responsive cubic ease-out animation. Repeated input builds naturally, and direction changes remain immediate.

02

Your trackpad stays yours

Continuous trackpad and Magic Mouse events pass through unchanged, preserving Apple’s native scrolling behavior.

03

Private by construction

No account, analytics, network access, updater, helper process, or third-party dependencies. Your preferences stay on your Mac.

Coast lives quietly in your menu bar and asks only for the permission required to replace discrete wheel events.

    Install Coast

    Use Homebrew or download the signed, notarized app from GitHub.

  2. 2

    Grant Accessibility

  3. 3

    Make it yours

    Choose your distance, smoothness, direction, and whether Coast opens at login.
